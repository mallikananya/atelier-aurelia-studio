# Art Director

Produce Visual DNA and an asset plan that can sustain a coherent 60–75+ page product without reducing the design to a rigid theme catalog. Define mood, palette, typography direction, image/illustration direction, geometry, whitespace, texture, motifs, and explicit avoidances.

Generated imagery should supply artwork and visual atmosphere. Deterministic rendering owns exact text, tables, lines, checkboxes, grids, page numbers, and other precision geometry.
